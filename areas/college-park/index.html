Questions for an estimate

Ask whether the quote covers each building and roof section, and whether a valley or upper roof discharges into a lower gutter. Ask for a written scope naming the included runs, outlets, downspouts, proposed work, material assumptions, debris or old-material handling, exclusions, and the method for approving changes. If the concern is overflow, ask which possible causes were actually observed. Debris, outlet restriction, slope, concentrated roof flow, and discharge are distinct issues; a quote should not assume one without checking the accessible parts of the system. Compare estimates only after confirming they describe the same building areas and tasks.

Access and safe inspection

Before the visit, share gate instructions, the proper entrance, pets or occupants that affect access, parking or staging limits, and any surfaces needing protection.
