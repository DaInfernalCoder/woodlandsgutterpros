5-inch or 6-inch?
Start with the roof.

The right gutter size depends on the roof area feeding each run, its shape, the rainfall design and the outlets. Width is one part of the decision.

5-inch gutters

A 5-inch profile may fit a suitable roof section. Confirm the collection area, outlet capacity, mounting surface and downspout arrangement before choosing it.

Explore 5-inch gutter options

6-inch gutters

A 6-inch profile provides a wider channel. It still needs compatible outlets, sound supports and an appropriate discharge route; extra width cannot clear a blockage.

Good questions before gutter work.

Do I need cleaning, repair or new gutters?

Start with the symptom and the roof section involved. Cleaning addresses debris; repair addresses a defined defect; installation or replacement changes the system. A property assessment can separate those needs before you compare estimates.

Are 6-inch gutters always better than 5-inch gutters?

The wider profile is one option, not a universal answer. The roof area, rainfall design, channel slope, outlets and downspouts must work together. A blocked outlet or damaged support needs attention regardless of gutter width.

Do gutter guards eliminate cleaning?

No. Guards can reduce some debris entering the channel, but leaves can collect above them and fine material can still reach the gutters. The guard design should include a practical way to inspect and maintain the system.

What should I prepare for a gutter estimate in The Woodlands?

Have the property location, number of stories, the roof edges involved and a description of any visible issue. Ground-level photographs can help explain a concern. Note gates, pets, landscaping and access restrictions; the actual scope is established at the property.
